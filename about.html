ABOUT

Hello there!
Im Tamara, a multidisciplinary creative with a background in graphic design, digital design, creative direction, and visual storytelling.

My work is driven by a love of thoughtful details, strong concepts, and creating visuals that feel both beautiful and intentional. I’m especially drawn to editorial design, branding, web design, fashion, and image-making, often blending romantic and ethereal influences with a modern, slightly unexpected edge. Whether I’m building a digital experience, developing a visual identity, photographing a concept, or bringing an idea from sketch to final execution, I love creating work that feels distinctive, cohesive, and memorable. Currently based in Vancouver, I’m continuing to expand my practice across design, art direction, photography, and creative projects.

I’ve been creating for as long as I can remember. After filling countless sketchbooks, my dad introduced me to digital art in the early 2000s. He built PCs and worked with Photoshop and I still remember him telling me:
“Tamara, don’t be scared. Explore. There is nothing you can break that I can’t fix.”

In 2023, I returned to school to pursue creativity professionally. Now, a year and a half into BCIT’s Flexible Learning Graphic Design and UI/UX program, I’ve expanded into graphic design, illustration, branding, retouching, compositing, and web design. I started the program intimidated by Illustrator; today, I’m creating vector artwork and designing and deploying live websites. Graphic Design

I create thoughtful, visually cohesive work across branding, print, editorial, and digital design, balancing strong concepts with refined details.

Digital Design

I design engaging digital experiences and visual content with a focus on clarity, usability, and a strong overall aesthetic.

Photography

I use photography as a storytelling tool, with a focus on fashion, portraiture, editorial imagery, and concept-driven visuals.

Creative Direction

I develop and shape creative concepts from the initial idea through to final execution, bringing together styling, design, imagery, colour, and typography into one cohesive vision.

Visual Storytelling

I use design, imagery, and narrative to create work that communicates a clear mood, message, and point of view across different mediums.
