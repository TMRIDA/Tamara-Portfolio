First Works

By tweleve, I was actively drawing in MSPaint, Photoshop and experimenting with animation.
At fourteen, inspired by the Magical Girl Anime Tokyo Mew Mew, I created my first full-length GIF featuring an original character of my own design. Mew Sumomo, drawing each frame individually. It was actually my second attempt, the first disappeared after my computer overheated and I learned, very quickly, the importance of saving properly!

At fitteen, I created Mew Yuki, another original character inspired by Tokyo Mew Mew.

And by sixteen, I enjyed creating custom characters so much I created Mew Bubblegum, another original character inspired by Tokyo Mew Mew.

Character design and storytelling have remained part of my creative work ever since, including my 2021 illustration of my orginal character Lumi.

My illustrations remain feminine and refined, while showcasing my ability to adapt seamlessly to a variety of styles, email to find out more. :)
